Sensor placement and calibration poses

N2: secure on the forearm just above the back of the wrist, not across the wrist joint or on the hand. N4: secure on the outer upper arm below the shoulder joint, moving with the upper arm. N3: your elbow-side position is auxiliary; it does not drive either avatar segment. The boards may face different directions. Keep the straps fixed throughout calibration and testing.

  1. Neutral: stand upright, face the screen, arm hanging straight down and relaxed. Hold still for 3 seconds.
  2. Side: point the straight right arm directly out to your side at shoulder height, palm facing the floor (this tells the model which way your palm faces). Keep your torso still and hold for 3 seconds.
  3. Forward: point the straight arm straight ahead of your shoulder at shoulder height. Palm orientation does not matter here. Hold for 3 seconds. The app measures where the arm points, so turning the palm cannot skew the calibration; the live readout shows the angle from your side hold (aim for ~90).
  4. Hinge (starts automatically): turn the palm up, keep the upper arm steady, and do 3 slow full curls. The measured curl peak is reported.

One wobble no longer cancels a hold: each pose keeps a rolling 3-second window. If a strap moves, redo calibration. Raw streaming records sensor and motion samples for Download Log; an inference session is not required.
